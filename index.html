UV LED & TiO₂

How it works

Breaks the breeding cycle.

Pehra doesn't just keep mosquitoes away for a night. It removes them from your space, before they can breed, in three quiet steps.

01 · Attract

The UV LED & TiO₂ coating

A UV LED shines on a titanium-dioxide coating, a reaction that mimics human breath. Mosquitoes follow it the way they'd follow you.

02 · Capture

The silent fan

As they come close, a whisper-quiet fan creates gentle suction and pulls them down through the grille. You won't hear a thing.

03 · Contain

The trap chamber

Inside the sealed chamber, mosquitoes dehydrate and die naturally, without sparks, mess or chemicals. Empty it whenever it's full.
